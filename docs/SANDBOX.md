# Sandbox: kill times

Generated by `node build.mjs` from `src/data/weapons.js`. Do not edit by hand.

Every pilot has 70 shield and 45 health. Shields absorb everything and damage past a broken shield carries into health. Headshots only count once shields are down, except on weapons marked *head always*. Precision weapons have no random spread: the shot goes exactly where the reticle is, moving or airborne.

Weapons marked *(lab)* are prototypes, off unless turned on in Settings → Labs (see `docs/PROPOSALS.md`). Shots for burst weapons count rounds; for pellet weapons, a shot is every pellet hitting at close range.

**Perfect** medal: a kill with a precision weapon in its minimum shot count with no missed shots in between (the magnum's is 4 body or head shots to break shields, then a headshot: 5 shots, 1.04 s).
